# RUKA privacy notice (draft, have it reviewed before launch)

**Who we are:** RUKA is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with EDISU Piemonte or Politecnico di Torino. Contact: [your email].

**What stays on your device:** your profile, saved summaries, chat history and settings are stored in your browser (localStorage). You can delete everything from Profile → "Delete my profile and data".

**What we collect on our servers:** nothing about you personally. The site host (Netlify) keeps standard technical logs (IP address, time) for security. Feedback and "report incorrect information" messages are stored only where the project team has set up a collection point [describe].

**AI help (optional, off until you agree):** if Ruka can't understand a question, and you agree, that question text is sent through our server to Mistral AI (France, EU) to interpret it. Before sending we remove emails, phone numbers and ID codes. We never send your profile, ISEE, CFU history beyond the minimal facts you stated, or documents. On the free plan Mistral may use prompts to improve its models. You can switch AI help off at any time from the footer link.

**Documents you paste or upload:** analysed in your browser. They are not sent anywhere in this beta.
